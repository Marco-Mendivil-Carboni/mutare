        \section{Introduction}

        % Nuclear \textbf{chromatin structure and dynamics} play an important role in processes such as \textbf{gene regulation} and cell fate decisions in developmental biology, as well as other processes such as epigenetic modifications \cite{Misteli2020}. This encourages us to study how they change when the nucleus deforms under \textbf{mechanical forces} and in particular to investigate \textbf{cell migration}, a fundamental physiological function that also plays a central role in pathological processes, particularly in \textbf{cancer metastasis} (in which cancer cells deform extensively through narrow constrictions to invade distant tissues). In this poster, we present and analyze a \textbf{polymeric model of chromatin} with the aim of understanding some of the changes in its structure and dynamics when the nucleus undergoes this kinds of deformations \cite{DeCorato2024}.
